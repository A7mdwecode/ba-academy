What Does a BA Do?
🔍

Analyze Data & Processes

BAs study how businesses work — finding what's broken, what works, and where opportunities are. Data, not gut feelings.

🤝

Bridge Business & Tech

Translate between non-technical stakeholders and developers. Write requirements, create mockups, keep everyone aligned.

📊

Build Dashboards & Reports

Using Power BI, Tableau, or SQL, BAs visualize data so leadership can make fast, smart decisions.

🎯

Drive Business Decisions

Every query, report, and recommendation has a real business impact. BAs turn raw data into direction.

Cloud BA (AWS / Azure)

Entry-level cloud. ~10 hrs study. Free prep on AWS.

High demand in UAE/Gulf. Validates BI skills.

Gold standard. Requires 2 years work experience.
